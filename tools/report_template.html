tickmatch: one simulated trading day

A hardware matching engine that follows Taiwan Stock Exchange rules, run in simulation for one symbol and one day. Every answer of the RTL on this day was compared, in order, with a software model of the rulebook, and they were identical.

Educational model of published TWSE rules; not affiliated with or endorsed by TWSE; not investment software. The order flow is generated from a fixed seed. No real order data is used.

Replay

Step through the day. Near the opening auction, the first interruptions and the close every message is kept; elsewhere the day is sampled. In call phases the line between asks and bids shows the price the auction would produce right now.
